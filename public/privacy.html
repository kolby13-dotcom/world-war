Privacy Policy

World War · Last updated November 26, 2026

The short version

We only keep what the game needs to work: your username, a scrambled copy of your password, your friends list, your game stats, and the profile photo and bio you choose to add. We don't show ads, don't use tracking or analytics, and never sell your information.

What we collect

  • Account: the username you pick and your password. The password is stored only as a one-way hash, so nobody (including us) can read it.
  • Profile: an optional photo (from your camera or gallery) and a short bio. Other players can see your username, photo, bio and stats.
  • Friends and rooms: who you add as a friend, and which game room you are in.
  • Game stats: games played and games won.
  • Chat: messages you send in a room or match are passed to the other players in it. They are not stored after the room closes.

We do not collect your email, phone number, location, contacts or any device identifiers. Camera and photo access is only used when you choose to set a profile photo, and only the picture you pick is uploaded, shrunk to a small square.

Where it is kept

The game runs on a hosted server (Render) and saves accounts in a hosted database (Neon). Both store data in the United States. Connections to the game are encrypted (HTTPS).

Practice mode

Practice games against bots run entirely on your device. Nothing from practice mode is sent to us.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the game: open Profile, tap Delete account, and type your password.
  • On the web: go to the account deletion page.

Deleting removes your account, password, profile photo, bio, stats and friends list right away. Nothing is kept afterwards.

Children

World War is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, delete it using the steps above or contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
